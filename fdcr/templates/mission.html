FDCR Mission

FDCR’s mission is to transform the diamond industry by promoting the highest standards of ethical practices, sustainability, and social responsibility. As an independent public search database, FDCR certifies lab-grown diamonds produced with sustainable energy and endorses alluvial diamonds collected through environmentally conscious practices, while ensuring fair labor conditions and safe work environments across the supply chain.

Unaffiliated with any labs, wholesalers, or dealers, FDCR verifies the legal import and distribution of diamonds, setting a new benchmark for transparency and accountability. By championing sustainability, fairness, and safety without engaging in diamond production, sourcing, or distribution FDCR strives to redefine ethical standards in the diamond trade, empowering consumers and stakeholders with confidence in their choices.
